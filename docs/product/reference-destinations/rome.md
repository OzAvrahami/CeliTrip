# CeliTrip — Rome Reference Destination

**Status:** Discovery
**Country:** Italy
**Destination:** Rome
**Last Research Pass:** August 2026
**Purpose:** Validate CeliTrip's destination, place, safety, evidence, and freshness models against real-world data.

---

## 1. Executive Finding

Rome is an excellent reference destination for CeliTrip.

Italy has unusually mature infrastructure for people with celiac disease.

The Italian Coeliac Association (AIC) operates the **Alimentazione Fuori Casa (AFC)** program, covering thousands of food-service establishments across Italy.

Participating businesses receive training and consultation and are periodically monitored.

However, an important limitation exists:

**The current venue directory is not openly available as a reusable public database.**

AIC members can access the directory, while international visitors can obtain temporary access through AIC's visitor offering.

### Product Implication

CeliTrip should not attempt to replace AIC as an accreditation authority.

Instead, CeliTrip should:

* Explain what AIC and AFC mean.
* Record AIC accreditation when it can be currently and independently confirmed.
* Direct users toward authoritative resources when appropriate.
* Maintain its own place and evidence database.
* Never infer current accreditation from historical evidence.

## 2. Destination-Level Assessment

### Celiac Infrastructure

**Discovery assessment: High**

Rome has:

* Dedicated gluten-free bakeries.
* Dedicated gluten-free restaurants.
* Restaurants with separate gluten-free kitchens.
* Specialist gluten-free grocery stores.
* AIC-trained establishments.
* Supermarkets with significant gluten-free selections.
* Gluten-free food availability at the airport.

Italy also has relatively strong general awareness of celiac disease.

### Product Decision

CeliTrip should not initially publish artificial numeric scores such as:

> Rome — 9.4/10 for celiac travelers

We currently have no methodology capable of defending such a score.

A qualitative description is more appropriate:

> **Strong celiac infrastructure**

A numerical destination rating may be introduced later if CeliTrip develops a transparent scoring methodology.

## 3. Gluten-Free Labelling in Italy

The primary Italian phrase travelers need to recognize is:

**Senza glutine**

EU food regulations allow a product to be described as gluten-free when it contains no more than 20 ppm of gluten.

Italy also uses the recognized Crossed Grain symbol.

AIC maintains its own food-product information and licensing programs.

### CeliTrip Must Distinguish Between

```text
EU-labelled Gluten Free
AIC Crossed Grain
AIC food directory listing
AIC AFC restaurant participation
```

These claims are related but are **not equivalent**.

## 5. Major Discovery — Evidence Is Not One Field

The PRD currently defines an `Evidence Level`.

Rome demonstrates that this model is probably too simplistic.

Consider this example:

```text
Voglia di Pizza

Evidence A
---------
Source: AIC Lazio
Observed: 2020
Claim: AFC Member
Authority: High
Freshness: Stale

Evidence B
---------
Source: Venue Website
Observed: 2026
Claim: Gluten-free menu available
Authority: Medium
Freshness: Current
```

What should the place's single `Evidence Level` be?

There is no correct answer.

### Better Model

A place should have multiple evidence observations:

```text
Place
  └── EvidenceObservation[]
```

Each observation could contain:

```text
type
claim
source
source_authority
observed_at
valid_from
valid_until
freshness
status
notes
```

CeliTrip can then derive its current confidence from all relevant observations.

This is much stronger than:

```text
place.evidence_level = "venue_confirmed"
```

---

## 6. Accreditation Must Be Time-Aware

Accreditation should not be modelled simply as:

```text
aic_accredited = true
```

Accreditation can change over time.

A better structure is:

```text
accreditation:
  authority: AIC
  program: AFC
  status: confirmed
  observed_at: 2026-08-xx
  source: ...
```

Historical evidence should remain preserved.

For example:

```text
AIC AFC

Confirmed:
2020

Current status:
Unknown
```

This is preferable to deleting the historical record or pretending it is still current.

## 7. Dedicated Gluten Free Is More Complex Than a Boolean

Rome also demonstrates that:

```text
dedicated_gluten_free = true / false
```

is probably insufficient.

A place may be:

* Entirely gluten free.
* A mixed restaurant with a separate GF kitchen.
* A GF bakery supplied by another production facility.
* A specialist GF shop with an internal dedicated laboratory.
* A mixed kitchen with defined cross-contact controls.

### Possible Facility Profile

```text
FULLY_GF

SEPARATE_GF_KITCHEN

SHARED_KITCHEN_WITH_CONTROLS

GF_PRODUCTS_ONLY

UNKNOWN
```

This should be considered during formal Data Model design.

## 8. Supermarkets and Shopping

Rome demonstrates that supermarket information should not simply be treated as restaurant-style Places.

Travelers need answers to two different questions:

> Where can I buy food?

and:

> What should I look for when I am inside the supermarket?

### CeliTrip Shopping Information

```text
Shopping in Italy

├── How to read labels
├── What "senza glutine" means
├── Crossed Grain symbol
├── Supermarket chains
├── Specialist GF stores
├── Common brands
└── Emergency food options
```

Examples of useful supermarket information include:

* Gluten-free sections.
* Own-brand GF ranges.
* Common GF brands.
* Specialist stores.
* Packaged emergency food.

Some of this content belongs at **Country level**, while location-specific information belongs at **Destination level**.

## 9. Airport

Rome Fiumicino Airport publicly indicates that gluten-free food is available at multiple food outlets.

However:

Availability of gluten-free food does **not** automatically establish celiac-safe preparation.

Therefore CeliTrip should display something such as:

> Gluten-free food is available at Fiumicino Airport.

followed by:

> Individual outlet cross-contact procedures have not been confirmed.

CeliTrip should **not** display:

> Fiumicino Airport is safe for celiac travelers.

### New Entity Discovery

We likely need a new domain concept:

```text
TravelHub
```

Examples:

```text
Airport
Train Station
Ferry Terminal
Motorway Stop
```

A Travel Hub can provide food guidance without itself being treated as a restaurant.

## 12. Freshness Model

Rome confirms that freshness should belong to individual claims rather than only to a Place.

This is too vague:

```text
Mama Eat

Last verified:
August 2026
```

What exactly was verified?

A better model is:

```text
Venue exists
Observed: August 2026

Separate GF kitchen
Source: Venue
Observed: August 2026

Separate pizza oven
Source: Venue
Observed: August 2026

AIC membership
Current independent status: Unknown

Opening hours
Observed: August 2026
```

### Product Principle

> Freshness belongs to the claim, not only to the place.

## 13. Source Authority

A preliminary hierarchy emerging from the Rome research:

```text
1. Accreditation Authority
   Examples:
   AIC
   Recognized national celiac organizations

2. Government / Regulatory Source

3. Venue Itself
   Useful for current operational claims

4. Recognized Institutional / Tourism Source

5. Recent Celiac Traveler Reports

6. General Community / Reviews

7. Search Result or Directory Without Supporting Evidence
```

However, authority alone is insufficient.

For example:

A highly authoritative source from 2020 may be less useful for answering:

> Is this restaurant accredited today?

than a lower-authority but current source.

Therefore confidence should eventually consider:

```text
Authority
×
Freshness
×
Claim Relevance
```

## 17. Recommended Changes to PRD v0.1

Rome produced four significant discoveries that should be incorporated into the next PRD version.

### Change 1 — Evidence

Replace the concept of:

```text
Single Evidence Level
```

with:

```text
Multiple Evidence Observations
```

A place can have evidence from multiple sources with different:

* Authority.
* Freshness.
* Claims.
* Confidence.

---

### Change 2 — Freshness

Replace:

> Places have verification dates.

with:

> Individual claims have observation and freshness dates.

---

### Change 3 — Dedicated GF

Do not initially model Dedicated Gluten Free as only:

```text
true / false
```

Different facility configurations must be represented.

---

### Change 4 — Travel Hub

Introduce:

```text
TravelHub
```

for entities such as:

* Airports.
* Train stations.
* Ferry terminals.
* Similar travel infrastructure.

## 18. Open Questions From Rome

1. What qualifies as sufficiently current AIC confirmation?

2. Can CeliTrip display AIC/AFC status and branding, and under what conditions?

3. What default expiry period should operational claims have?

4. Should venue claims expire after 3, 6, or 12 months?

5. How should conflicting current evidence be displayed?

6. Can users submit evidence without immediately changing a place's safety status?

7. How should CeliTrip distinguish a dedicated GF business from a dedicated GF production environment?

8. Should branches of the same restaurant chain share evidence or require branch-specific evidence?

9. Should supermarket chains be modelled as Places, Brands, Guides, or a combination?

10. Who should validate translated Celiac Cards before publication?

11. What happens when an authoritative but old source conflicts with a newer venue claim?

12. Which claims require independent verification before being displayed prominently?

---

## 19. Conclusion

Rome validates the core CeliTrip concept.

More importantly, it demonstrates that the product's differentiation should not simply be:

> Here are gluten-free places.

The stronger proposition is:

> Here is what we currently know about this place, why we believe it, how recent that information is, and what remains unknown.

That should become the foundation of the **CeliTrip Trust Model**.

---

## 20. Main Discovery Outcome

The Rome exercise revealed three modelling assumptions that should be changed before technical design begins.

### Evidence Level Is Too Simple

Evidence should be stored as individual observations.

### Last Verified Is Too Simple

Freshness should be associated with individual claims.

### Dedicated GF Boolean Is Too Simple

Facility configuration needs a richer model.

These discoveries should be incorporated into **PRD v0.2** before Technical Discovery begins.
